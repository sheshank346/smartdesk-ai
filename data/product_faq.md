# CloudCRM Product FAQ

## What is CloudCRM?
CloudCRM is a cloud-based customer relationship management platform that helps sales and support teams track leads, manage customer accounts, and automate follow-ups. It is designed for small and mid-sized businesses that need enterprise-grade CRM features without complex setup.

## What are the pricing plans?
CloudCRM offers three plans. The Starter plan costs 999 rupees per user per month and includes contact management, basic pipelines, and email tracking. The Professional plan costs 2499 rupees per user per month and adds workflow automation, custom reports, and third-party integrations. The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and includes dedicated support, advanced security controls, and API access with higher rate limits.

## Does CloudCRM offer a free trial?
Yes, every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to Professional plan features. No credit card is required to start the trial. At the end of 14 days, you can choose a plan or your account moves to a limited free tier with a cap of 100 contacts.

## How do I integrate CloudCRM with email?
CloudCRM integrates natively with Gmail and Outlook. Go to Settings, then Integrations, then Email Sync, and authenticate using OAuth. Once connected, all emails with existing contacts are automatically logged to their timeline within 5 minutes.

## Can I import data from another CRM?
Yes. CloudCRM supports CSV import and has one-click migration tools for Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho. Go to Settings, then Data Import, then choose your source CRM. Most migrations for under 10,000 records complete within 30 minutes.

## What security certifications does CloudCRM have?
CloudCRM is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Enterprise plan customers can also enable single sign-on (SSO) via SAML.

## How does workflow automation work?
Workflow automation lets you trigger actions based on events, such as sending a follow-up email when a lead status changes to Qualified, or assigning a new lead to a sales rep automatically based on region. Automations are available on the Professional and Enterprise plans and are configured through a drag-and-drop builder under the Automations tab.

## What is the API rate limit?
The Starter and Professional plans allow 1000 API calls per hour. The Enterprise plan allows 10,000 API calls per hour, with the option to request a higher custom limit for high-volume integrations.

## How do I raise a support ticket?
You can raise a support ticket from the Help Center inside the app, or by emailing support@cloudcrm.example. Starter plan customers receive email support with a 24-hour response time. Professional plan customers get priority email and chat support with a 4-hour response time. Enterprise customers get a dedicated support engineer and 1-hour response time for critical issues.

## How can I check the status of my support ticket?
You can check your ticket status by providing your ticket ID (format TCK-XXXX) either in the Help Center or by asking the support assistant directly.

## Does CloudCRM support mobile apps?
Yes, CloudCRM has native iOS and Android apps that sync in real time with the web platform. All core features are available on mobile except advanced reporting, which is web-only.

## Can I customize the sales pipeline stages?
Yes, pipeline stages are fully customizable on the Professional and Enterprise plans. Go to Settings, then Pipelines, then Edit Stages, to add, remove, or reorder stages to match your sales process.

## What happens if I cancel my subscription?
If you cancel, your account remains active until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, your account moves to the free tier with a 100-contact limit, and data beyond that limit is archived (not deleted) for 90 days, after which it may be permanently removed.

## Does CloudCRM offer role-based access control?
Yes. Admins can assign roles such as Admin, Manager, and Sales Rep, each with different permission levels for viewing, editing, or deleting records. Custom roles with granular permissions are available on the Enterprise plan.
